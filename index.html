About Us

Local land knowledge, built for Texas ground.

Texas Land Restoration works exclusively with Central Texas landowners — ranch owners, homesteaders, and developers who need overgrown or unusable acreage turned into land they can actually walk, build on, or graze.

We know the terrain: thick cedar breaks, caliche and limestone, seasonal brush growth, and the drainage patterns that come with Hill Country ground. Every project is scoped on-site before a single machine moves.

  • Free on-site walkthrough before every estimate
  • Equipment matched to your terrain, not a one-size approach
  • Clear, written scope before work begins — no surprise add-ons

Services Offered

Four ways we restore your land

Every property is different, so we scope each job around your goals — whether that’s pasture, a home site, trails, or fire mitigation.

Land Clearing

Land Clearing

Full-acreage clearing for home sites, pasture, or development — brush, small trees, and debris removed and hauled or mulched on site.
Forestry Mulching

Forestry Mulching

Low-impact mulching grinds brush and small trees into ground cover in a single pass — no burn piles, less erosion, faster turnaround.
Rock Chipping

Rock Chipping

Purpose-built for rocky, caliche-heavy ground — breaking down rock and brush together where standard mulching equipment can’t reach.
Cedar Removal

Cedar Removal

Selective or full removal of invasive Ashe juniper (“cedar”) to restore grassland, protect water yield, and reduce wildfire fuel.
